Scripture Scout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Scripture Scout ("the app") is a Bible verse and trivia game. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control or remove it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, and a username and avatar you choose. If you sign in with Google (or, in the future, Apple), we receive the basic profile information those services share for sign-in, such as your name and email address.
  • Gameplay data: your scores, streaks, difficulty settings, and progress through Story Mode and other game modes.
  • Social features: friend connections you create, challenges you send or receive, and messages you send through in-app chat.
  • Push notifications: if you enable notifications, we store a device token used to deliver them (for example, a challenge alert or a daily reminder to play).

How we use this information

We use this information only to operate the app's features: showing your scores on leaderboards, tracking streaks, connecting you with friends, delivering challenge and reminder notifications, and letting you sign back in.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, our database and authentication provider, stores all app data on our behalf.
  • Google (and, in the future, Apple), if you choose to sign in that way.
  • We do not share your information with any other third party, except where required by law.

Your choices

You can play without an account at any time by choosing "Continue without account" — in that mode, no personal information is collected, but scores and progress aren't saved between sessions.

If you have an account, you can permanently delete it — along with your scores, streaks, friends, and all associated data — at any time from Profile → Delete Account. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

Scripture Scout is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us using the information below and we will delete it.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee perfect security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
