Scripture Scout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Scripture Scout ("the app") is a Bible verse and trivia game. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control or remove it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, and a username and avatar you choose. If you sign in with Google (or, in the future, Apple), we receive the basic profile information those services share for sign-in, such as your name and email address.
  • Gameplay data: your scores, streaks, difficulty settings, and progress through Story Mode and other game modes.
  • Age confirmation: when you create an account (or the first time you sign in after we added this step), we ask for your birth month and year to confirm you are at least 13. This is stored privately, is not shown on your profile, and is not visible to other users.
  • Social features: friend connections you create, challenges you send or receive, messages you send through in-app chat, emoji reactions, and the users you block.
  • Reports: if you report a user or a message, we store your report, the reason and details you give, and the reported message's text so we can review it.
  • Push notifications: if you enable notifications, we store a device token used to deliver them (for example, a challenge alert or a daily reminder to play).
  • On your device: the app saves settings and your sign-in session in your device's local storage, for example your sound preference and dark mode.

How we use this information

We use this information only to operate the app's features: showing your scores on leaderboards, tracking streaks, connecting you with friends, delivering challenge and reminder notifications, and letting you sign back in.

We also use it to keep the community safe: chat messages are checked automatically for language we don't allow, and we review reports. If someone is found to be misusing the app, we may remove content or accounts.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking across other apps and sites.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, our database and authentication provider, stores all app data on our behalf.
  • Google (and, in the future, Apple), if you choose to sign in that way. On Android, push notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, which receives your device token and the notification text.
  • Content delivery services: to load, the app fetches fonts from Google Fonts and a code library from jsDelivr. These services can see your IP address and device type, as with any website request. We don't send them any account information.
  • Buy Me a Coffee: the optional "Support Scripture Scout" link (not shown in the iOS app) opens that site, which has its own privacy policy. Donations are handled entirely by them and we don't receive your payment details.
  • Other players can see your username, avatar, scores and streaks, and the messages you send them. We do not share your information with any other third party, except where required by law.

Your choices

You can play without an account at any time by choosing "Continue without account" — in that mode, no personal information is collected, but scores and progress aren't saved between sessions.

If you have an account, you can permanently delete it — along with your scores, streaks, friends, and all associated data — at any time from Profile → Delete Account. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

Scripture Scout accounts are for people 13 and older. We ask for your birth month and year when you create an account, and if you are under 13 we do not create the account, or we delete it if it already exists. Children under 13 can still play without an account, in which case no personal information is collected. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, please contact us using the information below and we will delete it.

Retention

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, scores, streaks, friends, messages, notification tokens, and age confirmation. Reports you filed, and reports filed about you, are removed along with the account.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee perfect security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We'll make any significant change visible in the app.
