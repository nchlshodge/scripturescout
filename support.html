Scripture Scout — Support

Need help with Scripture Scout? Start with the questions below, or reach out directly — we're happy to help.

My scores or progress disappeared

Progress only saves when you're signed in. If you played as a guest ("Continue without account"), scores and streaks aren't saved between sessions — sign up for a free account to keep your progress permanently.

I'm not receiving notifications

Check that notifications are enabled both in the app (Profile → Enable Notifications) and in your device's system settings for Scripture Scout. If you recently reinstalled the app, you may need to re-enable notifications once.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your scores, streaks, friends, and messages, and cannot be undone.

I found a bug or a question is wrong

We appreciate the report — please email us with as much detail as you can (what game mode, what happened, and a screenshot if possible).

Report inappropriate behavior

If another player is harassing you or misusing chat/friends features, email us with their username and details — we take this seriously and will investigate.
